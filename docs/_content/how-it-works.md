**01 — Retrieve.** A question is matched against a curated corpus of publications, datasets, and working documents.

**02 — Augment.** The most relevant passages are attached to the question as context for the language model.

**03 — Generate.** The model writes an answer drawn from that context, with citations back to the original sources.
